Curriculum and study details

Follow a proven path from the basics to advanced projects. See exactly what awaits you.

01

Design Fundamentals and Theory

You will learn the principles of composition, color psychology (including the secrets of gradients!), and typography. You will learn to think like a designer before even opening a program.

02

Practical Coding (HTML5 & CSS3)

You will turn theory into real-world projects. You will learn to create responsive layouts, advanced transformations, modern glass effects, and custom glitter-dusted backgrounds.

03

Interaction and Logic (JavaScript)

You will bring your web pages to life. We will focus on dynamic elements, whiteboard-style writing micro-animations, and real-time control of interface elements.
